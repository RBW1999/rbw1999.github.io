**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the **"QR-Code Scanner"** application developed by **Rafael Wortmann** for Meta devices.

### 1. Information We Collect and How We Process It

To function correctly, the app uses the OpenXR SpatialMarkerTracking API and requires the **Spatial Data Permission**.

When the app detects a SpatialMarker (QR code) in your environment, it temporarily reads the following data:

- **Position and Size:** Used to accurately place the QR code user interface (UI) in your virtual space.
- **Marker Type and Content:** Used to enable app features, such as allowing you to copy plain text or directly open detected URLs.

**Purpose of Processing:** All spatial and marker data is processed locally in real-time solely to provide the core functionality of the app (scanning and interacting with QR codes).

### 2. Data Storage and Third-Party Collection

**We do not store your data.** The app is strictly offline with zero external data collection. The content and spatial data of the scanned QR codes are **not stored** within the app, on your device, or on any external servers. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

### 3. Global Data Deletion Rights

Because we do not collect, store, or transmit any user or device data to external servers, we do not hold any personal data tied to you. However, all users globally have the right to request data deletion.

To submit a data deletion request or privacy inquiry, please email **info@rafaelwortmann.de**. We will promptly address your request and confirm that no personal data is being held.

### 4. Safety Disclaimer and Third-Party Links

The app allows you to scan QR codes that may contain URLs leading to third-party websites. **The content of scanned QR codes is not checked or screened for safety issues or malicious content.**

The app developer, Rafael Wortmann, has no control over the websites opened via scanned QR codes and takes no responsibility for the safety, content, or privacy practices of those external sites. Users open web links entirely at their own risk.
